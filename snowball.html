How to play

  1. Today's topic is something from your life. Write one short, true sentence about it — 3 to 8 words. I drink coffee.
  2. Add five layers, one at a time: when, where, why, who with, but… and so on. Each time, rewrite the whole sentence with the new part inside it. Move things around if it sounds better that way.
  3. After five layers you have one long, real sentence about your life. Share it — the share text includes the sentence, so you can send it to your teacher.

Each layer starts at 10 points. If the game can't spot the layer in your sentence, it loses 2 — fix the sentence, or tell the game it's there.

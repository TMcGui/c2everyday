How to play

Five expressions a day. For each one you get the meaning — guess the expression Americans actually say. The fewer guesses, the more points:

101st guess82nd63rd44th25th

A perfect day is 50. Every wrong guess unlocks the next hint:

  1. Where you'd hear it
  2. A real sentence with the expression blanked out
  3. The first letter of each word
  4. All of its vowels

The tiles show which words you got right:

my Right word, right place
bad In the expression, different place
fault Not in the expression

After each one you get a clip of real people saying it. Five new expressions arrive every day at midnight.

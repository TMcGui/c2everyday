How to play

Guess the idiom or expression in 4 tries. Every wrong guess unlocks the next hint:

  1. How many words it has
  2. What it means, and whether it's casual, formal, or modern slang
  3. Its form (verb phrase, noun phrase, single word…)
  4. All of its vowels

The tiles show which words you got right:

bite Right word, right place
the In the expression, different place
dust Not in the expression

Win or lose, you get a clip of real people using it. A new expression arrives every day at midnight.
